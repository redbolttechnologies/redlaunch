Quick start

Run on your VPS after installing Docker Engine, Compose, and the host tools.

Installation guide

The installer asks for an installation directory, Google OAuth credentials, and your first authorized email. The default directory is /opt/redlaunch.

sh · on the VPS
wget https://raw.githubusercontent.com/redbolttechnologies/redlaunch/master/install.sh
bash install.sh

On your computer, replace your-user@your-server and open an SSH tunnel. Keep it running while you use http://localhost:8080, then sign in with your authorized Google account.

sh · on your computer
ssh -N -L 8080:127.0.0.1:8080 your-user@your-server
